# Visual assets

- `orca-logo.png` is rendered from the official Orca project logo under the
  [MIT License](https://github.com/stablyai/orca/blob/main/LICENSE).
- `monitor.png`, `terminal.png`, `cloud-sun.png`, and `chevron-right.png` are
  rasterized from the matching [Lucide](https://lucide.dev/) outline icons
  under the ISC License.

The PNGs are sized for this app's 320×240 LVGL surface and retain transparent
backgrounds.
